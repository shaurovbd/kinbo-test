**Product Name:** Kinbo

**Version:** v1.0 MVP

**Author:** Product Team

**Status:** Draft

---

# 1. Product Vision

Kinbo is an AI-powered collaborative shopping list and household planning platform that makes grocery shopping simple, organized, and enjoyable.

Instead of sending shopping lists through WhatsApp or remembering items mentally, families can create shared shopping lists, collaborate in real time, receive AI recommendations, track budgets, and complete shopping faster.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

People experience several common problems:

* Shopping lists become outdated.
* Family members buy duplicate items.
* Important items are forgotten.
* Grocery budgets are exceeded.
* Communication through messaging apps is messy.
* No intelligent shopping assistant exists for everyday households.

Kinbo solves these problems through real-time collaboration and AI.
